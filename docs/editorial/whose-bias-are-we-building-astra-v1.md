Decision: GO. No required substantive revision. The writer and evaluator have substantive agreement on this draft. This is an editorial and source judgment, not verification of publication or site rendering.

I read the actual draft without the writer's self-review, inspected the proposed cover, and independently checked source clauses. Body length is about 1,260 whitespace-delimited words, with no em dashes. It meets the agreed short macro scope.

## Editorial judgment

The essay now offers a useful argument beyond the familiar observation that AI has values. Safety boundaries distribute capability; institutional access and government purchasing give those judgments economic force. The economic mechanism is concrete enough to matter without invented estimates. Amodei's proposed parity of AI advice in adverse government actions supplies an especially useful counterexample: capability access can restrain concentrated power too.

The opening earns its place. It is plainly imaginary, acknowledges substantive animal and environmental ethics, and distinguishes a specialized product from a depended-on general assistant. The chicken line is mildly comic without identifying a real CEO. Cyber follows quickly and does not equate grocery assistance with dangerous capability.

The political passage treats the administration's neutrality definition as a contestable purchasing rule without claiming that truth-seeking is undesirable. The lab passages acknowledge both their explicit principles and their stated support for outside oversight. The author still makes a claim after those concessions, so the nuance does not empty out the argument.

The ecology paragraph is proportionate. It illustrates why affected outsiders matter and remains hypothetical. It does not turn into an unrelated environment section.

The successor section distinguishes current assisted work from autonomous RSI and factual evidence from normative agreement. It does not call model evaluation useless, assume values remain unchanged, or treat a second model as institutionally independent. The closing standard supplies a defensible position rather than a technical checklist. It remains a normative proposal, as it should.

The main limitation is originality of degree: contestability and accountability are established governance ideas. The essay does not discover them. Its contribution is the accessible connection between legitimate safety, allocation of capability, commercial conditions, and a successor's lack of an automatic mandate. That is enough for this short essay and should not be advertised as a novel theory.
